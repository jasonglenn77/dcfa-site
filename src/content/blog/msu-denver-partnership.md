Direct Connect Flight Academy is proud to partner with **Metropolitan State University
of Denver (MSU Denver)** — a partnership that shortens the road to an airline career for
degree-seeking pilots, and one that earned national coverage in *Flying* magazine.

## What the partnership means for students

Through this partnership, MSU Denver's professional pilot students train with DCFA and
qualify for a **Restricted Airline Transport Pilot (R-ATP) certificate at 1,000 flight
hours** — instead of the standard 1,500. That's a full **one-year head start** toward the
flight deck.

MSU Denver became **Colorado's only four-year institution with FAA-approved R-ATP
eligibility**, and the pathway is designed to be flexible:

- Students can begin flight training at a partner community college, complete two years
  there, and **transfer to MSU Denver while keeping their R-ATP eligibility**.
- The combined degree-plus-flight-training path gets graduates to the regional airlines
  sooner, with a four-year degree in hand.
